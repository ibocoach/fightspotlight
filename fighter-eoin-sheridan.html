Profile

Eoin Sheridan is an undefeated Irish welterweight known as "The Sheriff." He has a strong kickboxing background and became the ISKA Irish National Kickboxing Champion in 2017. He has competed successfully in MMA and is currently undefeated as a professional. He has also trained with high-level fighters and has been a sparring partner of Conor McGregor.

About Fight Spotlight

Fight Spotlight is a combat-sports media platform dedicated to fighters, fights, events and the stories behind them.

Fight Spotlight brings combat sports closer to the fans. We cover MMA, boxing and other combat sports with a focus on the athletes, the competition and the moments that matter.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to give fighters and combat sports the attention they deserve, while giving fans a place to discover news, fighters, events and stories from the world of combat sports.

What We Cover

MMA

Fighter profiles, fight news, results, matchups and in-depth coverage from the world of mixed martial arts.

Boxing

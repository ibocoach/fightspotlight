Early Life & Beginning of the Journey

Born in February 1998 in Morocco, Brahim Bydamen discovered the world of combat sports at a young age. Growing up with a strong passion for martial arts, he began building the foundation of a fighter's mindset: discipline, dedication, and a constant desire to improve.

In 2016, a new chapter began when life took him to Ukraine for his studies. At only 18 years old, Brahim entered the world of MMA and began his journey toward becoming a professional fighter. Representing Rage Academy Zaporizhzhia, he dedicated countless hours to training and quickly established himself as one of the best fighters in his region.

Rise Through Amateur MMA

Brahim competed in several amateur tournaments, including regional championships in Zaporizhzhia Oblast and national MMA competitions across Ukraine. His determination and talent led him to become Champion of Zaporizhzhia Oblast and later Ukrainian MMA Champion in 2019.

That same year, he achieved another major milestone by earning the prestigious title of Master of Sport of Ukraine in MMA.

The Injury & The Hardest Years

However, every fighter's path is tested by adversity.

While preparing for his third professional fight in Ukraine, Brahim suffered a serious arm injury that forced him away from competition. From 2020 until 2023, he faced one of the hardest battles of his career: the battle outside the cage against injury, uncertainty, and the long road to recovery.

The Comeback in Germany

After moving to Germany, Brahim refused to let his dream fade. With the same determination that had shaped his career, he returned to training and competition.

Representing No Mercy Fight Club Moers, he competed in Germany, the Netherlands, and France.

His comeback reached another major milestone when he captured the title of German Champion (Deutscher Meister), proving that true fighters are defined not only by their victories but also by their ability to rise after every fall.

Professional Career

Competing in the welterweight division at 170 lb, Brahim has built an impressive professional MMA record of 8 wins, 1 loss, and 0 draws (8–1–0).

Mentor & Coach

Today, Brahim continues his journey not only as a professional fighter but also as a mentor. He dedicates his experience, knowledge, and the lessons learned through years of competition to the next generation of athletes.

By coaching young fighters, he helps them pursue the same dream he once had and teaches them that success is built through patience, sacrifice, discipline, and belief.

The Journey Continues

Brahim Bydamen's story is more than a record of victories. It is a story of ambition, resilience, and the unbreakable spirit of a fighter who turned every obstacle into another step toward greatness.

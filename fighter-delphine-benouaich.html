Profile

Delphine Benouaich is a French strawweight fighter known as "Benphine." Her foundation comes from grappling, and she later developed her MMA and boxing skills. She built a strong amateur résumé before turning professional and currently has a 7-2 professional record.

  • Nationality: France
  • Team: Mahmoudi Gym

Background

Delphine began with grappling and competed internationally before moving further into MMA and boxing. She has represented France in amateur competition. She also works as a physical education teacher.

Current Exposure

Delphine Benouaich is a contestant on The Ultimate Fighter 34, Team Cormier.

Major Achievements

  • 🥉 IMMAF European Championships — Bronze, 2022
  • 🥈 IMMAF World Championship — Silver, 2023
  • 🏆 French MMA Champion — 2023
  • 🏆 Cage Warriors Academy Champion
  • 🥊 French boxing achievements

Recent Fight

Defeated Lindsey Payne by guillotine choke submission, Round 1 at ARES 36 on December 13, 2025.

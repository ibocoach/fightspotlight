Profile

Gemma Auld is a Scottish flyweight fighter from Kilmarnock. She brings a background in rugby and Dutch kickboxing to MMA and entered professional MMA after a successful 6-0 amateur career. She started her professional career with two consecutive victories in PFL competition.

  • Fighting Out Of: Kilmarnock, Scotland

  • Age: 35

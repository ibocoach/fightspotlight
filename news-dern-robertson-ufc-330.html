Dern Defeats Robertson in First Strawweight Title Defense

Mackenzie Dern controlled Gillian Robertson on the mat for four rounds to complete the first successful defence of her strawweight title in Philadelphia.

UFC·15 Aug 2026·UFC 330·By Fight Spotlight

Mackenzie Dern is still the UFC women's strawweight champion. In the co-main event of UFC 330 at Xfinity Mobile Arena in Philadelphia on 15 August 2026, Dern defeated Gillian Robertson by unanimous decision — 49-46, 49-46 and 48-47 — in the first defence of her title. According to the UFC, the result extended her winning streak to four.

A Grappler's Duel

The matchup brought together two of the most accomplished grapplers in the division, and many expected a cautious, technical contest on the mat. Instead, Dern imposed herself early. She took Robertson down repeatedly, passed her guard and spent long periods in dominant positions.

The first round nearly ended the fight. Dern sank in a deep rear-naked choke at the end of the opening five minutes, and Robertson survived only as the round expired.

Control Over Four Rounds

The champion's dominance on the ground continued through the middle of the fight. Reports noted that Robertson conceded four of six takedown attempts and that Dern accumulated more than ten minutes of control time. Robertson, a highly decorated grappler in her own right, rarely found a way to reverse position or threaten from underneath.

Robertson's Fifth-Round Surge

The final round was a different story. Dern appeared to tire, and Robertson pushed hard for the finish she needed, producing her best work of the night with her striking and ending the fight with Dern on the bottom. She took the round on the scorecards, but by then the champion's lead was secure.

Fight Spotlight Analysis

This was a clear and deserved victory, built on Dern's world-class grappling. Against a fellow submission specialist, she was able to take the fight where she wanted, keep it there and dictate positions — a strong indicator of how far her overall game has developed since winning the title.

At the same time, the performance left room for improvement, and Dern herself reportedly acknowledged she would have liked to find the finish. The near-submission in the first round showed the opportunity was there, and her drop in output in the fifth gave Robertson a late foothold. Future challengers will study that final round closely.

The contest also underlined how much Dern's wrestling has improved. Taking down and holding a grappler of Robertson's quality requires more than submission skill, and Dern's ability to secure top position repeatedly was the foundation of everything else she achieved. That combination of takedowns and elite jiu-jitsu makes her a very difficult stylistic puzzle for the rest of the division.

For Robertson, the defeat came against one of the few grapplers in the division who could match her strengths. Her late rally showed resilience, and she remains a dangerous opponent for anyone at 115 pounds.

For the champion, a first successful defence is an important milestone. Holding the belt is one challenge; keeping it against a top contender is another. Dern has now done both, and the strawweight division's leading names will be lining up to test whether her grappling can be solved.

In the UFC 330 main event, Islam Makhachev retained the welterweight title and set a UFC record with his 17th straight win.
